# Evidence: the `Gated` Movement Pipeline

Measurements of plan [`2026-11-27-gated-movement`](../plans/2026-11-27-gated-movement.md). `WP0a`'s figures are in
the plan's Amendment 1 (§A1.3). This file starts with `WP0b`, the base of record every later package is judged

binaries, assets per arm, worlds, scripts, logs and the analysis tables.

## 1. WP0b: method

- **Tree.** Branch `gated-wp0b`, cut from `main` `2f922a50`, with friend-crowd's latent C-3′ and the follow order
  (schema 50) on it. Its code commits are `5681882d` (the crowd-cap selector, §1.1) and `fc5d07b5` (the `lanes`
  correction, plan Amendment 2). `main` moved four times while the base was measured: `c3c372c6` (launch and spell visibility, schema 53), `b1dbb43f` (weapon delivery, schema 56), `4ff8402f` (Carrier interceptors, schema 57) and `156721c2` (missile movers, B-52, schema 60). Each is merged, the last one as the final tree. §6 shows which worlds moved: only the missile movers moved any, battles 12c, 19, 35 and 36b and the mirror, and those were re-measured on the final tree. The rest of the base is byte-identical there. The throughput of §7 is measured on the final tree.
- **Arms** (§6.1). All three are played from one binary per tree, selected by data:
  - **L** is `Legacy`;
  - **S** is `Solver` as shipped (`crowd_iterations: 21`, C-3′ off);
  - **R** is `Solver` with `contact.crowd_iterations: 151` (C-3′ on, the Q0124 reference).

  The matrix, battles, clashes, showcases and throughput read a scratch catalog directory per arm
  (`assets/{L,S,R}`): the shipped `assets/` with only `pipeline` or `crowd_iterations` edited. The harness takes
  `--pipeline legacy|solver` and, for R, `--crowd-iterations 151`.
- **Binaries.** These are release builds, each in a target directory of its own:
  - `matchup_trace`, `movement_harness` and `route_trace` of the head (`/home/spacescuffle/cargo-target/gated-wp0b`);
  - `main` `2f922a50` exported (`…/gated-wp0b-main`), for identity;
  - the merged tree (`…/gated-wp0b-merged`);
  - one single-scenario throughput binary per scenario of the final tree (`…/gated-wp0b-bench`);
  - the scratch B-225 probe (`…/gated-wp0b-probe`, §5).
- **Runs** (`scripts/measure.sh`). Each run is played alone under `flock ~/scratch/gate.lock`, with `--jobs 6`:
  - **harness:** the 13 scene families on the development range, seeds 1 to 1,000. The five older scene families
    are also relabelled (seeds 1 to 1,000). The mirror runs on seeds 1 to 1,000. The permutation mode
    (`--permute 7`) covers all 13 families and the mirror on seeds 1 to 1,000;
  - **matrix:** the 61 battles on the four seeds of record and the tool's first 20 others (`--seeds 20`: 24 seeds);
  - **battles:** battle 31 on its seeds of record plus 100, and battle 33 on its seeds of record plus 64;
  - **clashes:** the two 400-unit clashes (`pulse_bot` and the zergling analogue `razor_mantis`) on the
    benchmark's seed 7 and seeds 1 to 4;
  - **showcases:** the showcase and showcase-effects scenarios on the default seed and seeds 1 to 8, 1,500 ticks.

  Harness verdicts are `movement_harness --aggregate` (T1 to T9 and the known answers K). Matrix figures come from
  `scripts/matrix_base.py`. "Beyond noise" is the candidate's per-seed mean outside the base's 95 % bootstrap
  interval of the per-seed mean (2,000 resamples, seed 7), on the worse side.
- **Seeds registered and not run** (§6.1, S-10). Nothing in this file reads them:
  - harness acceptance range: **300,001 to 301,000**;
  - harness held-out range: **9,100,001 to 9,101,000**;
  - matrix held-out seeds: **the tool's 45th to 68th non-record seeds**, at `WP4` only.

### 1.1 The crowd-cap selector (`5681882d`)

The `WP0a` hand-back noted that the harness selected a pipeline but not C-3′'s cap. `RunOptions::with_crowd_iterations`
sets `contact.crowd_iterations` and no other rule, validated as the catalog validates it. The CLI takes
`--crowd-iterations <cap>`; without the flag the options are untouched.

Three tests in `crates/sim_fixtures/tests/harness_tests.rs` cover it. They do not compile on `2f922a50` (the API is
new), which is their red run:

| Test | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `the_crowd_cap_selector_sets_the_cap_and_nothing_else` | the cap reaches the built world; caps below `iterations` or above 1,000 are refused |
| `the_shipped_crowd_cap_plays_identically_and_a_raised_cap_extends_the_solve` | the shipped cap through the selector gives the shipped rules exactly and the same CSV; cap 151 extends gap seed 2's solve past 21 iterations |
| `the_command_line_takes_a_crowd_cap` | the flag parses into the play command |

In the base, R's solver ran 151 iterations at most and 4.85 on average (S: 21 at most, 4.44 on average) over 11.48
million harness ticks.
