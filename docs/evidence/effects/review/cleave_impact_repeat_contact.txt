Effect contact sheet of 'cleave_impact' (plan 2026-11-09-effect-visualization §6.5), made by
`battle_capture --effect-lab cleave_impact` on the production effect plugin and shaders.
Bindings: SplashFull=0.5, SplashMid=1, SplashReach=1.5. Lifetime: 2.750 s. Capture 1280x720, battle camera at 58° pitch.
Camera: shipped camera; the effect spawned on the same spot several times as an attacker's consecutive hits (`--repeat`), each with its own instance seed as consecutive events in a battle have; the lifetime runs from the first to the end of the last.
cleave_impact_repeat_contact.png columns: ages 0 (0.000 s), 0.03 (0.082 s), 0.08 (0.220 s), 0.15 (0.413 s), 0.3 (0.825 s), 0.5 (1.375 s), 0.75 (2.062 s), 1 (2.750 s).

close zoom, its reference, dark default, its reference, dark close, its reference.
Default-zoom cells show the middle half of the frame, close-zoom cells its middle three quarters
(a line's close-zoom cells the whole frame).

40 ms, read left to right, top to bottom.
