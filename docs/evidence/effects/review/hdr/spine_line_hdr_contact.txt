Effect contact sheet of 'spine_line' (plan 2026-11-09-effect-visualization §6.5), made by
`battle_capture --effect-lab spine_line` on the production effect plugin and shaders.
Bindings: SplashFull=0.5, SplashMid=0.5, SplashReach=0.5, LineLength=8, LineSpacing=1. Lifetime: 2.500 s. Capture 1280x720, battle camera at 58° pitch.
Camera: HDR camera with bloom above 1.0 (`--hdr-bloom`, plan D-11 experiment).
spine_line_hdr_contact.png columns: ages 0 (0.000 s), 0.03 (0.075 s), 0.08 (0.200 s), 0.15 (0.375 s), 0.3 (0.750 s), 0.5 (1.250 s), 0.75 (1.875 s), 1 (2.500 s).
Rows: bright ground default zoom, the same with the 1-unit grid and the bound radii (red full
damage, yellow reach; for a line, the line and the full radius at each search point), bright
close zoom, its reference, dark default, its reference, dark close, its reference.
Default-zoom cells show the middle half of the frame, close-zoom cells its middle three quarters
(a line's close-zoom cells the whole frame).
spine_line_hdr_sequence.png: the first 3 s on the bright ground at the default zoom, one frame every
40 ms, read left to right, top to bottom.
Driver: lavapipe, debug build, 2026-11-10, effects.ron of commit 1eaaf64 (round 0 data, ramps up to 6.0).
Its LDR counterpart for the A/B is the same program without --hdr-bloom on lavapipe (scratch
frames; statistics in ../hdr_bloom_decision.md); ldr_hdr_ab_close.png shows the close cells side by side.
