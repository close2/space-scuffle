Crowd capture of the effect stress mode (plan 2026-11-09-effect-visualization §7.2 item 6, §10.3), WP6c.

Made by `frame_budget --scenario 400 --display-path kwin-virtual --effect-stress 200 --capture-tick 150
--capture <png>` (release build, RX 580 / radv, headless KWin, 1280x720, 2026-11-10), and the same command
without `--effect-stress` for the baseline. The battle: eight armies of 50 units on a flat 128x128 map, each
attack-moving to the opposite start, seen from the frame-budget camera (75 world units away, 58 degree pitch).
The stream: 200 splash events per second of game time, round-robin over shell_impact, acid_burst and
spine_line, each at a live unit chosen by a hash, with the zones (and line) the simulation publishes.

stress_capture_400.png: with the stream, simulation tick 150 (6 s into the battle, 1 200 events sent so far).
stress_capture_400_baseline.png: the same tick without the stream (the simulation is deterministic, so the
units stand in the same places).
Both are cropped to x 360..1280 of the 1280x720 window: the left 360 pixels are the HUD's side panel, which
the headless capture shows black in both runs.
