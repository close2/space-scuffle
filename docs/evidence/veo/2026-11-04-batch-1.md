## Review method

Each clip got both sheets from `asset_baker review` (the 16:9 sheet and the framed sheet). They are committed here
under `review/` with the operation id in the name, so the WP3 pilot's sheets keep their names. Each clip was checked
against the plan's D-7 criteria, as amended in `a463dc6` with the owner's two review points.

- **Loop continuity.** Three measurements and one visual check, all with `/usr/bin/ffmpeg`:
  1. SSIM (all channels) between the first and last decoded frames at full 1280×720;
  2. for comparison, the median SSIM between consecutive frames sampled at 8 fps, about one atlas step (4 s / 32
     frames). A seamless loop should come close to that value;
  3. a camera-zoom estimate. The last frame, at 640×360, is rescaled by a factor s from 0.80 to 1.06 with small
     offsets (±20 px). The s whose centre 400×220 window best matches the first frame's window (SSIM) is kept;
     s < 1 means the subject grew by about 1/s;
  4. a visual check of the first and last frames side by side, and of a 2×2 grid at 0, 1, 2 and 3 s.
- **Backdrop** (owner, relayed 2026-11-04): a plain, uniform backdrop with no radiating stripes, sunburst, light
  rays, spotlight beams or patterned gradient. A clip that fails this is rejected.

**Rays.** The pilot and the first two regenerations all have a purple sunburst behind the dome. The cause was the
style sentence's "radial studio backdrop". No keyframe was involved: those three clips were text-to-video. With the
plain-backdrop sentence and the negative prompt, both later clips have a flat, even backdrop. Frame 0 of `0dbd7948`:
[`review/plasma_siphon_dome_idle_0dbd7948_frame0_plain_backdrop.png`](review/plasma_siphon_dome_idle_0dbd7948_frame0_plain_backdrop.png).

**Camera.** All five clips push in by about 7 to 10 % over 4 s. This happened with no camera wording, with a
locked-off clause at the end, with a static-tripod clause at the start, with a negative prompt naming zoom, dolly
and push-in, and with image-to-video from a still frame. Prompt wording does not control it for
`veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`, so further regeneration would spend money without a reason to expect a different
result. The plan's retry allowance for batch 1 is 3 regenerations, and 4 were used, under the owner's "do not
hesitate to regenerate". WP4 therefore stops here and asks Q0046.
