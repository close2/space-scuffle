## Review method

Each clip got both sheets from `asset_baker review` (the 16:9 sheet and the framed sheet). They are committed here
under `review/` with the operation id in the name, so the WP3 pilot's sheets keep their names. Each clip was checked
against the plan's D-7 criteria, as amended in `a463dc6` with the owner's two review points.

- **Loop continuity.** Three measurements and one visual check, all with `/usr/bin/ffmpeg`:
  1. SSIM (all channels) between the first and last decoded frames at full 1280×720;
  2. for comparison, the median SSIM between consecutive frames sampled at 8 fps, about one atlas step (4 s / 32
     frames). A seamless loop should come close to that value;
  3. a camera-zoom estimate. The last frame, at 640×360, is rescaled by a factor s from 0.80 to 1.06 with small
     offsets (±20 px). The s whose centre 400×220 window best matches the first frame's window (SSIM) is kept;
     s < 1 means the subject grew by about 1/s;
  4. a visual check of the first and last frames side by side, and of a 2×2 grid at 0, 1, 2 and 3 s.
- **Backdrop** (owner, relayed 2026-11-04): a plain, uniform backdrop with no radiating stripes, sunburst, light
  rays, spotlight beams or patterned gradient. A clip that fails this is rejected.

## Decision: the paid `0dbd7948` is not accepted as the Idle; the WP4 pilot goes ahead (seamless-loops WP3)

The question was whether the already-paid plain-backdrop clip `0dbd7948`, repaired, could be the
`plasma_siphon_dome` Idle instead of the WP4 pilot generation. The owner said on 2026-11-04, relayed by the
orchestrator: "we have a lot of credits. rescuing clips is good, but don't hesitate to use the credits (they will
expire in 2 months anyway) if you think you should get a better result". So a rescued clip is accepted only if it is
as good as a fresh pinned generation would likely be.

It was reviewed against every written D-7 criterion, on the 16:9 sheet, the framed sheet and loop-check's repaired

| Action readable at 512 px | pass, weakly: rising steam and pulsing plasma |

**Verdict: not accepted.** The repair leaves a visible artefact at the restart, the very place the loop is meant to
hide. A fresh generation pinned at both ends (`image` = `lastFrame` = frame 0 of `0dbd7948`) is expected to need
little or no repair, so it should be crisper and free of double images. It costs one generation ($0.40); the owner
asked for credits to be used when they give a better result. **Recommendation: run the WP4 pilot as planned.**
`0dbd7948` stays rejected in the ledger and remains WP4's fallback only in the case the plan names (a `lastFrame`
refusal). In that case its ghosting must be weighed again, for example against a cross-fade of 0 frames.
Nothing was installed or baked. The running total is unchanged: 5 generations, $2.00.
