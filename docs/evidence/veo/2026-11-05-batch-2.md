## Review method

As batch 1: `asset_baker review` (the 16:9 and framed sheets), `loop-check` for the looping states (raw and
repaired, both sheets; the welder's Walk and Cast measured against their start pictures with `--pin`), a 3 × 3
detail sheet of source frames 0, 12, …, 84, 95, and an ffprobe check of the sound. A Constructing clip is compared
with its start picture at its first and last source frames (SSIM, all channels, full size). Where a thin part could
flicker, a crop of it every 8 frames at 2× was added (`_roof`, `_spires`). All sheets are under [`review/`](review/)
as `<slug>_<state>_<clip>{,_framed,_detail,_loopcheck_repaired,_loopcheck_seam}`.

The worker's atlas keeps 8 frames per state (32 over four states), so one loop step is 0.5 s and the median step
SSIM of a moving unit is low (0.6 to 0.75 against 0.92 to 0.99 for a still building). The threshold is relative to
the median (worst ≥ step − 0.03), so it still applies.

## Deviations from the plan, and why

- **`foundry_core_dome` Idle regenerated, not kept.** D-6 and the seamless-loops plan D-3 keep the legacy Idle and
  pin the Constructing on its frame 0. Reviewed under D-7, the legacy clip fails: loop-check FAIL (worst 0.767 <
  0.882, a jump at kept frame 4→5), the yellow gantry arms move, the camera zooms in mid-clip, and it is
  pillarboxed with black bars inside its 16:9 frame (Veo kept the square keyframe and padded it), so neither the
  clip nor its frame 0 meets the plain-backdrop rule. It is removed (`9f132d4`; in git history), the identity got a
  seed and a pinned Idle (2 + 1 generations), and its provenance is `Veo` for both states instead of `LegacyVeo`.
  The legacy `foundry_core_dome_walk.mp4` and `_attack.mp4` stay in `assets/videos`; no atlas uses them now (a
  structure publishes neither state).
- **The square legacy keyframes `welder_drone_idle.jpg` and `foundry_core_dome_idle.jpg` are removed.** D-7 names
  them as start images, but the loops plan made Idle pinned at both ends, and a square cannot pin; the `.jpg` would
  also shadow a 16:9 `.png` pin. The welder was seeded from text with a prompt describing one design.
- **`welder_drone` Cast has its own pin** (`welder_drone_cast.png`, a real frame of its first Cast clip) instead
  of the Idle picture (loops plan D-3). The Cast loop shows a lit arc; a clip pinned to the arc-less Idle picture
  must light and quench the arc inside the loop, which loop-check rejects. The switch from Idle to Cast therefore
  lights the arc at once, which reads as starting to weld.
- **`welder_drone` is letterboxed** (framing override), as the shuttle: the torch held out to one side spans about
  68 % of the 16:9 width and the centre crop cut it off.
