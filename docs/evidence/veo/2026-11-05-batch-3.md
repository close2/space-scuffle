## Review method

As batches 1 and 2: `asset_baker review` (16:9 and framed sheets), `loop-check` for Idle and Walk (raw and repaired,
both sheets; pinned clips against their pin), a 3 × 3 detail sheet of source frames 0, 12, …, 84, 95, and an ffprobe
check of the sound. Every sheet was looked at for the owner's backdrop rule (no radiating stripes, sunburst, rays or
spotlight beams). Sheets are under [`review/`](review/) as
`<slug>_<state>_<clip>{,_framed,_detail,_loopcheck_repaired,_loopcheck_seam}`. With three published states each
unit's atlas keeps 10 frames per state (one step 0.4 s), and the loop thresholds are those of the loops plan D-6
(worst ≥ median step − 0.03, |growth| ≤ 1.5 %, start fidelity ≥ 0.90).

  remaining units (batch 5) can reuse the overrides' wording once it is proven on them.
