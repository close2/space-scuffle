## Batch 3: the rethink of the two open Walks (Q0020, visual decisions)

**`railgun_crawler`: redesigned, option (b) of batch 3's list.** Option (a) could not help (the side flames are in
every frame of the old pin). Option (c), a Walk that reads as the Idle with a rumble, was rejected under the owner's
guidance for this session: a clip must show the motion its state names, and a crawler Walk is acceptable only if
its tracks or body clearly convey movement. The old pin's side exhaust flames flickered in all five Walks, so no
Walk built on that design could loop cleanly. The identity was redesigned from text with "four exposed chunky
tracks, smooth armored hull sides without side vents, two exhaust stacks at the rear", and the Walk text asks for
"all four tracks run fast and continuously, treads and road wheels visibly moving the whole time … exhaust smoke
streams backward". The accepted Idle (`6eedaf3a`) and Attack (`63a361fc`) of the old design were withdrawn in
the ledger (`rejected_in_review`, "withdrawn after acceptance") and removed from `assets/videos` with the old pin
(they stay in git history), so the three states show one design.

The new Walk (`6c7b5798`) passes loop-check on the first attempt. Its tracks visibly run in the source clip: the
tread links of the top run shift about a third of a link per source frame
([`_tracks`](review/railgun_crawler_walk_6c7b5798_tracks.jpg), source frames 0 to 7 and 24 to 95), the hull
vibrates and the stack smoke streams backward. **Known limit, recorded:** the atlas keeps 10 frames per state, one
every 0.4 s, and at that step the tread phase changes by a fraction of a link
([`_tracks_sampled`](review/railgun_crawler_walk_6c7b5798_tracks_sampled.png), the sampled source frames 0, 10, 19,
29, 38, 48), so in the game the tracks read as a shimmer rather than a smooth run; the travel itself comes from the
unit's movement, as for the shuttle. A smaller frame size would keep more frames (the bake's own note); that is a
bake setting for all units, outside this batch.

**`shock_golem`: Walk end left free.** Pinned at both ends to its wide-stance Idle picture, eight Walks (six in
batch 3, two here) barely stepped, stepped unevenly, leaned toward the camera or drew a disc under a foot. The
rethink changes two things, each recorded in `media_style.ron`:

1. The Walk's end is no longer pinned (`loops.overrides`, `Walk: (pin_end: false, …)`); the start stays the Idle
   picture (fidelity 0.993), and the baker's loop sampling picks the loop.
2. The text asks for "a brisk march on the spot as if on a treadmill: four full steps, left, right, left, right,
   each knee lifting high", with "nothing else appears in the frame".

The accepted Walk (`c9924231`) alternates its feet through the clip with no drift (growth +1.38 %) and no added
object. With the default cross-fade of 2 frames the seam showed a double image of the head and cannons
([`_crossfade_ghost`](review/shock_golem_walk_c9924231_crossfade_ghost.png)), so its seam is loop sampling only
(`crossfade_frames: 0`, `scale_lock: false`), as the specter's and the hound's Walks; the committed loop-check
sheets are those of the shipped treatment.

## Review method

As batches 1 to 3: `asset_baker review` (16:9 and framed sheets), `loop-check` for Idle and Walk (raw and repaired,
both sheets; pinned clips against their pin), a 3 × 3 detail sheet of source frames 0, 12, …, 84, 95, and an ffprobe
check of the sound. A Constructing clip's first and last source frames are compared with its start picture (SSIM,
all channels). Every sheet was looked at for the owner's backdrop rule (no radiating stripes, sunburst, rays or
spotlight beams); two seeds broke it (`planetary_bulwark_dome` `45e6e7c5`, `flak_array_dome` `bfb11f3a`) and no
accepted clip does. Sheets are under [`review/`](review/) as
`<slug>_<state>_<clip>{,_framed,_detail,_loopcheck_repaired,_loopcheck_seam}`.

**A pattern worth knowing.** Four pinned structure Idles (mechanics, arsenal, fusion and the bulwark, seed 1)
breathed: the camera pushed in by 1.5 to 2.8 % mid-clip and back out to the pin, which the scale lock cannot undo.
All four pins came from seeds with a steady push-in. Each passed with seed 2 and the same wording, so the seed, not
the text, decides it; the next batch can start pinned structure Idles on two seeds at once.
