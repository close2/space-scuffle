## Review method

As batches 1 to 4: `asset_baker review` (16:9 and framed sheets), `loop-check` for Idle and Walk (raw and repaired,
both sheets; pinned clips and end-free clips started on a pin measured against it with `--pin`), a 3 × 3 detail
sheet of source frames 0, 12, …, 84, 95, and an ffprobe check of the sound. Where the worst step needed explaining,
source frames on either side of it were compared side by side, and consecutive-frame SSIM was measured over the
whole clip. Every sheet was looked at for the owner's backdrop rule (no radiating stripes, sunburst, rays or
spotlight beams): one seed broke it (`sky_bastion` `a25e084f`, diagonal light beams), and two trike Walks drew faint
speed-line streaks; no accepted clip does. Sheets are under [`review/`](review/) as
`<slug>_<state>_<clip>{,_framed,_detail,_loopcheck_repaired,_loopcheck_seam}`.

  their end left free (the golem's Walk treatment). They are data for the next attempt; no accepted clip uses them.

  fire in the Idle, and the pin is the first seed with none. Its Attack still fires a jet of flame.
- **Two seeds of one pinned Idle could not run at once** (above); they ran back to back.
- **`grav_harvester` and `nectar_worker` Cast were not started.** Neither identity has an Idle clip or pin, a Cast
  pin must come from a first Cast clip chained from one, and a Cast alone cannot be baked (the atlas needs every
  published state). They belong to a batch that makes those identities whole.
