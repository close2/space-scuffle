## The rethink

**Trike: re-seeded from text, option (a) of batch 5.** The twin-barrel pin's fine painterly detail and cyan outline
glow shimmered in all ten of batch 5's Idles, and its wheels never turned in three Walks. The authored Idle text now
asks for "bold clean armor plates, no outline glow, three wheels only … with cyan-lit spoked hubs", and the trike's
Idle override for a parked, switched-off trike whose wheels are locked. The accepted Attack `3b2a9cc2` (installed
SHA-256 `61ff588b…`) started and ended on the retired pin, so it was withdrawn in the ledger (`rejected_in_review`,
"withdrawn after acceptance") and removed from `assets/videos` with the old pin (both stay in git history), as
batch 4 did for the crawler.

**Mech: a new pin with the arms lowered.** The held-out arm-cannons shifted in all nine of batch 5's Idles. The
authored Idle text now asks for "its two massive arm-cannons hanging low at its sides, muzzles pointing forward and
down … centred with empty space all around it, no floor, no shadow"; the Idle override for a mech that "stands still
… keeping its three-quarter view: it never turns toward the camera"; the Walk override names "never turns, leans or
steps toward the camera" first; and the Attack override raises both cannons, fires and lowers them again. The
accepted Attack `12eec7cc` (installed SHA-256 `48df4b0c…`) was withdrawn with the old pin, as the trike's.

**Loop treatment, both identities** (`media_style.ron`, explained there). Batch 5 had left the end of both Idles and
both Walks free. Each new pin came from a seed whose camera moved (the mech's pushed in by 12 %), and with the end free
the clips on it kept that motion (mech Idles +6.2 % and +12.2 %, mech Walks +2.9 % and +8.0 %, trike Idle −1.5 %, trike
Walk +2.5 %). All four are pinned at both ends again: both Idles take the default treatment; the trike's Walk keeps no
scale lock with the default cross-fade, the mech's Walk loop sampling only.

## Authored texts checked against the per-kind rules (before any spend)

Every wording was validated (`validate-prompts`, which also enforces the 1024-character limit) before the run that
used it. Rewordings from written rejections:

- **Trike, sparks and floor.** "A closed engine with no sparks … floating in empty space with no floor and no shadow"
  after seed A1 drew a sparking energy core and a lit floor band.
- **Trike, colour.** "Glowing cyan spoked hubs" after A2 came out colourless grey.
- **Trike, stage beams.** Three seeds of the "glowing cyan" wording (A3 to A5) drew diagonal or vertical light beams
  across the backdrop, the owner's rule. "Cyan-lit spoked hubs … floating in evenly lit empty space" drew none.

  four-wheeled buggy.

  crept round; "its wheels never spin", then "parked and switched off … its wheels are locked still with steady hub
  lights" brought the loop within reach, and seed 3 of that wording passed.
- **Trike Walk, the hull.** "The hull vibrates on its suspension" gave an uneven bob; "the hull rides steady and
  level" passed with the wheels still visibly turning.
- **Mech, framing.** "Centred with empty space all around it" after A2's feet touched the bottom edge.
- **Mech Idle and Walk, the facing.** "Never turns toward the camera" (Idle), "its chest and feet keep facing the
  lower right of the frame" and finally "marks time on the spot without travelling: short heavy steps" (Walk).

## Review method

As batches 1 to 5: `asset_baker review` (16:9 and framed sheets), `loop-check` for Idle and Walk (raw and repaired,
both sheets; clips on a pin measured against it with `--pin`, seeds with `--no-pin`), a 3 × 3 detail sheet of source
frames 0, 12, …, 84, 95, and an ffprobe check of the sound. Where the worst step needed explaining, source frames
around it were compared side by side; for the trike's Walks, crops of the front and rear wheels over eight
consecutive source frames checked that the wheels turn. Every sheet was looked at for the owner's backdrop rule (no
radiating stripes, sunburst, rays, spotlight beams or speed lines): three trike seeds broke it (`c037c8d6`,
`bb614078`, `8d52c702`, light beams) and no accepted clip does. Sheets are under [`review/`](review/) as
`<slug>_<state>_<clip>{,_framed,_detail,_loopcheck_repaired,_loopcheck_seam}`.
