## Scope: which states

The states come from the code the baker and the client share: `UnitCatalog::publishable_actions`
(`crates/sim_core/src/published_actions.rs`) gives Idle always, Walk for a unit that can move, and Attack for a mobile
unit that can ever be armed, which includes an armed mode (`ground_weapon_override` of the `burrowed` mode); the
scorpion has no harvester or construct capability, so no Cast. `AnimState` has no burrowed or morph action, and the
client presents the burrowed mode only through the snapshot's `cloaked` flag (the shimmer over the same atlas). So the
atlas holds Idle, Walk and Attack, 10 frames each.

**Gap, recorded:** the client shows no burrow or unburrow morph and no distinct burrowed pose. A burrowed scorpion is
drawn as the standing one, cloaked, and its Attack plays the standing strike (a row of bone spines bursting from the
ground ahead of it), which reads as the Lurker's spine line. A burrowed presentation would need a new published action
and client support; it is out of scope here.

## Authored texts checked against the per-kind rules (before any spend)

The authored texts named the ground ("digging into loose soil", "parting dust", "erupting from ground"), which drew
floor planes in batches 2 and 5, and a burrowing change of shape. They were rewritten in `units.ron` as one whole
beast seen from above at three-quarter with no floor; `media_style.ron` gained overrides for the three states, the
letterbox (feather 30 ‰) from the first clip, Attack pinned at its end to the Idle picture, and a Walk without scale
lock (all explained in the file). Every wording was validated (`validate-prompts`, 1024-character limit) before the
run that used it. Rewordings from written rejections:

- **View and floor.** "Seen from high above at a three-quarter angle … floating in evenly lit empty space" after seed
  A1 drew a side view on a lit floor.
- **Organic look.** "Organic scorpion beast of thick brown chitin" after A2 drew a grey metal robot, off the Swarm's
  bioluminescent look.
- **Still Idle.** "Crouches as still as a statue: body, legs, claws and tail never move" after two Idles flexed the
  claws.
- **Treadmill Walk.** "Scuttles on a treadmill that holds it in place … it never moves forward, back or toward the
  camera" after two Walks crawled back and forth in the frame (growth −3.3 %, −5.6 %); then "its six legs clearly
  lift and step" after a Walk on the second pin barely moved its legs.
- **Re-seed, compact crouch.** The first pin's spread claws and raised legs read as a pose in motion: five Idles and
  five Walks on it failed (claws and legs shift; the claws' swing read as a ±5 % scale change). The authored Idle text
  now asks for a crouch "low and compact: six short legs tucked close under its body, two broad pincer claws folded
  shut in front of its head, a spined tail curled over its back".
- **Smooth backdrop.** "The smooth plain backdrop stays unchanged" in the Walk and Attack after clips on the second pin
  turned its smooth backdrop into a grainy sand texture.

## Review method

As batches 1 to 5b: `asset_baker review` (16:9 and framed sheets), `loop-check` for Idle and Walk (raw and repaired,
both sheets; clips on a pin measured against it with `--pin`, seeds with `--no-pin`), a 3 × 3 detail sheet of source
frames 0, 12, …, 84, 95, and an ffprobe check of the sound. Where the worst step needed explaining, source frames
around it were compared in a 2 × 2 crop. Every sheet was looked at for the owner's backdrop rule (no radiating
stripes, sunburst, rays, spotlight beams or speed lines): no clip broke it. Sheets are under [`review/`](review/) as
`burrow_scorpion_<state>_<clip>{,_framed,_detail,_loopcheck_repaired,_loopcheck_seam}`.
