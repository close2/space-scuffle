## Projection and foreground placement

The current `ownership/geometry.rs::ProjectionView::bounds` projects all eight transformed AABB corners, then applies the gameplay ROI. The amendment correctly separates raw screen bounds from ROI admission: an obstacle partly outside the gameplay ROI must still participate in overlap checks. Preserve that separation explicitly.

- Validate finite AABB center/half extents, nonnegative half extents, propagated transform and camera/projection inputs **before** reducing corners with min/max. Floating-point min/max can suppress NaNs rather than reliably carrying an invalid corner into the final result. Known nonfinite/invalid bounds are errors, not pending readiness or an absent obstacle.
- Query `LabPreviewBubble`/`MistRibbon` identities first with optional bounds/transform/visibility components. A required-component query silently omits relevant identities while their bounds are missing. Missing or not-yet-propagated data must remain pending under the existing wall/frame deadlines, not contribute a zero extent. A loaded Lab shell must exist.
- A transformed AABB's world-Z maximum must use all transformed corners (equivalently the exact affine support formula), not local half extent plus translation; shells are scaled and effects/ribbons can be rotated. The current corner projection method is appropriate for a completely projectable convex box.
- The locked Bevy `world_to_viewport` rejects corners outside its depth domain. For a visible near-plane-intersecting box, an unprojectable corner does not mean the obstacle is absent. The proposed conservative rejection is acceptable; do not drop failed corners and retain a falsely small rectangle. A general clipped-box projection is not required for this capture scope.
- Physical screenshot coordinates remain `(viewport-local logical projection + logical viewport origin) * target scale`. The existing `world_to_window` / `ViewportCoordinates` helpers implement this. Do not use egui points-per-pixel or omit the nonzero viewport origin. Include a non-unit scale/nonzero viewport origin test, alongside entirely off-ROI and partially visible obstacle rectangles.
- Define the12-pixel minimum precisely. Recommended conventional rectangle distance: `dx=max(B.min.x-A.max.x, A.min.x-B.max.x, 0)`, similarly fordy, gap=`hypot(dx,dy)`. This gives zero for touching/overlapping rectangles and permits exact12. An axis-only gap is a different stricter policy; choose/document one and test diagonal separation too. Compare raw finite rectangles, not integer-rounded or viewport-clipped versions, and record the actual minimum pair/obstacle identity.

Coordinate spaces need an explicit conversion: `CombatEventVisual` positions are `[map_x,map_y,altitude]`; Bevy world positions are `(map_x,altitude,map_y)`. The foremost **world-Z** stage therefore changes event index1, not altitude index2. The fixed camera currently approaches from positive worldZ; its distance14 and derived ground-plane focus must remain the existing declared capture policy. Do not present a maximum-worldZ construction as a general camera-independent front-depth proof.

Compute and retain the admitted event stage/focus once after required extent readiness. Once lightning is emitted, later40ms aging advances Lab motion/mist and can change their bounds. Recomputing a new event origin or moving the camera for middle/late samples would undermine the same-event/fixed-stage evidence. Keep the original stage and recheck current visible obstacle separation at every phase; a later overlap is an explicit failure. Do not move the effect to rescue a later image.

The4-world-unit staging gap establishes an arrangement, not the12-pixel separation or visual coverage by itself. The screen test must still check all actual visible shells/ribbons. Numeric rectangle separation proves their conservative geometry is separated; it does not prove a strobing shader drew pixels or approve art quality.

## Material/shader time must survive asynchronous readback

A concrete existing architecture gap matters to the new multi-phase claim. `driver::readback` currently verifies simulation tick/checksum, while `OwnershipState::observe` permits ordinary decreases in effect remaining time. Thus an accidental extra40ms during a pending screenshot can leave simulation unchanged and still pass that generic callback check, even though saved phase metadata and actual rendered pixels disagree.

The amendment should retain a phase proof through every Settle/Readback observation and the callback boundary: actual effect identity, material identity, owner/seed, timer elapsed/duration and material age must remain the admitted values once the target is reached. Preserve the captured shader time as well. This can be a narrow ownership-only readback invariant; it need not redesign the generic screenshot mechanism. A test that injects age after verification but before delayed callback must fail, including when the authoritative checksum is unchanged. Multiple frozen readback frames must pass with no additional age.

The actual combat shader strobe uses Bevy `globals.time`. The locked render `prepare_globals_buffer` writes `Time::elapsed_secs_wrapped()` (f32), not the existing manifest's raw `elapsed_secs_f64()`. Record that wrapped shader-facing value as well as the ordinary presentation seconds, plus exact seed bits or a checked finite seed. This permits independent auditing of `floor(globals.time*60)+seed`, including a wrap boundary, without claiming CPU evaluation is GPU pixel evidence. Material handle and owner equality alone do not establish seed immutability.

All three requested images must be fresh and retained, including legitimate OFF phases. Three fixed ages do not mathematically guarantee a visible strobe sample; if every phase is off, the declared visual acceptance must fail. Never choose a fourth favorable time or retune the seed/lifetime. At least one inspectably visible image is a separate reviewer judgment after exact17-goal callback/identity contracts pass.
