## Implemented contract

Production uses immutable integer topology per pool slot, one shared material, three persistent device buffers, and a bounded mapped staging ring. The complete packet contains64-byte emitter headers,32-byte chronological world-position/relative-age samples and an80-byte shared frame. Default512×48 capacity transfers819,280 bytes. Packet selection freezes before material queueing; the render graph records its three copies before camera passes; main-world publication is acknowledged only after renderer submission. Stalled staging retains geometry, age, style and time from the previous complete packet. Slot generations gate main-world visibility and are checked again against actually queued identities.

Vertices reconstruct world geometry once. Main transforms retain the acknowledged emitter origin for bounds; bounds include both pending and retained published support. Per-view transparent distances are corrected to the selected packet's emitter origin before phase sorting. No mesh/material geometry assets are rewritten each frame. Main emission/history behavior and fragment art are preserved.
